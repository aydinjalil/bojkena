A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Clear answers.

I’m not a medical professional. Can I take a class?

Yes. Community CPR, Heartsaver, and emergency skills workshops are intended for people without a medical background. BLS and ACLS serve healthcare professionals and others with relevant professional requirements.

Which class do I need for work or school?

Ask your employer or school for the exact course name and accepted issuing organization. Share that requirement with us so we can confirm the appropriate class before you book.

Can I arrange a private or group session?

Individual and group training are part of the Be The One offering. Tell us your preferred course, approximate group size, location, and availability in the inquiry planner below.

Will I receive a certification?

Certification courses require successful completion of the applicable course and skills requirements. The issuing organization, card type, and completion process will be confirmed before enrollment. Emergency skills workshops are separate from certification courses unless explicitly stated.

Does drowning response training include lifeguard certification?

No. Emergency response workshops focus on awareness and response skills. They are not lifeguard certification, swimming instruction, or in-water rescue training.

Where can I see prices and available dates?

Pricing, locations, and the class calendar are being finalized for launch. This draft lets you explore the offerings and prepare an inquiry; it does not take payments or reserve a place.
